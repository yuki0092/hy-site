Privacy Policy

App: LunaMixer: Per-App Volume (Japanese: ルナミキサー) / Publisher: HY Studio / Last updated: 22 September 2026

日本語

This policy sets out how the mobile app "LunaMixer" (the "app") handles user information.

1. Information collected and stored

The developer (HY Studio) never receives any information about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no email address to enter. The app's own code never sends usage data or a list of your installed apps anywhere.

The only things the app stores on your device are the items below. They live in the app's private storage and cannot be read by other apps. They are excluded from cloud backup.

  • The theme you picked (colour and light/dark)
  • Whether first-run setup (pairing) has been completed
  • Whether the app has been purchased (see "3. Purchase and trial")
  • When the trial started
  • How many days the app has been opened, and whether the rating prompt has been shown
  • The key and certificate used to connect to the device's own wireless debugging (used only on this device)

Per-app volumes are not stored; they return to normal when volume control is stopped.

2. Network use

The app shows no adverts. The developer has built in no analytics and no crash reporting.

The app does hold Android's internet permission (INTERNET). It is needed to reach Android's own wireless debugging endpoint. That endpoint is on the same device the app is running on; changing volumes never connects to any external server.

In-app purchases use Google Play's billing system, and the Google Play Billing Library built into the app does contact Google's servers. That traffic is generated by the Google Play Billing Library; the developer never receives its contents. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Wi-Fi is required during first-run setup because Android only makes wireless debugging available while a Wi-Fi connection is active. That Wi-Fi does not have to reach the internet.

3. Purchase and trial

Every feature is available for three days from the first connection. Continuing to use the app after that requires a single one-off purchase through Google Play. There is no subscription.

  • Payment is processed by Google Play. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers.
  • The app asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made and stores the answer on the device. The purchase carries over to a new phone on the same Google account.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's rules.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — to show the volume bubble and panel while you are using other apps.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — the six-digit pairing code shown during first-run setup stops working the moment you leave that screen, so you type it into a notification instead. Notifications also show that volume control is running. The code is used to connect and is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE and related) — to keep the bubble on screen while other apps are in use.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to detect whether you are on Wi-Fi, so setup can say so.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — as described under "2. Network use".
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — as described under "3. Purchase and trial".

The name and icon of each app that is playing audio come from the app's own helper process, started through wireless debugging, and are used for display only. The app does not use the permission to query installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES), and it does not use Android's Accessibility service.

5. Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The developer holds nothing about you, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.

The purchase record is held by Google Play against your Google account. The pairing registered on your device during setup can be removed by you at any time under Settings → Developer options → Wireless debugging.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Disclaimer

The app is a tool that sets per-app volume using Android's own developer features.

  • Using the app requires Android's "Developer options" and "Wireless debugging" to be switched on. Please decide for yourself whether leaving those on is appropriate for your device. In particular, if you leave "USB debugging" on, take care not to connect to computers or USB devices you do not trust.
  • Some apps produce sound in a way that cannot be adjusted this way. Operation with every app is not guaranteed.
  • Setup steps and behaviour may differ depending on the device model, Android version, and manufacturer changes.
  • The developer accepts no liability for any damage arising from use of the app, except in cases of wilful misconduct or gross negligence.
